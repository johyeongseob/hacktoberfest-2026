## Cursor Pro Account

The challenge asked me to install the Cursor desktop app, redeem the MLH promo code, upgrade to Cursor Pro in Billing, and submit a dashboard screenshot showing Pro.

1. **Claiming the code.** My first claim failed with "pool is inactive or expired": the codes had run out. MLH later added codes restricted to existing MLH community members (to block spam accounts), and the second claim worked. Claiming also required linking GitHub to my MLH account.
2. **Redeeming.** The code added a **$34.00 credit** to my Cursor account.
3. **Upgrading.** Cursor's standalone Pro plan no longer exists. Both "Upgrade to Pro" in the desktop app and "Upgrade to Premium" on the web open the new **X Subscription** checkout, a unified plan for Cursor, Grok, and X:
   - Plans: Plus, **Premium** (the former Pro, "$20/mo" in the app), and Super.
   - X and Grok accounts are optional to connect, but a Grok account record is created in the background, and **account linking is permanent** after checkout.
   - The first month is discounted to half price, and the full monthly price applies from the second month. The first-month charge is billed to the card and then refunded from the Cursor credit; the remainder moves to the xAI account as credit.
4. **Result.** I subscribed to Premium. The first-month charge was refunded right away by the credit, and the desktop app's Plan & Usage page shows **Current plan: Premium**, which I submitted to both Cursor cards along with the claimed promo code page.
5. **Cleanup.** I canceled the subscription immediately; it stays active until the end of the billing period with no renewal. I did not buy extra usage credits, so third-party models (e.g. Claude) stay disabled rather than billed.s
